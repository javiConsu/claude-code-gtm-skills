5 Skill Categories for Outbound

Reusable capabilities — built once, called indefinitely.

Skill 01
ICP Research & Validation
Score any company against your ICP in seconds, not hours of manual research.
I say "Validate this company against our ICP"
Claude Pulls site, checks team size, identifies tech stack, scores fit. Returns a one-line verdict with reasoning in under 30 seconds.
Skill 02
Signal Detection
Find companies with an active reason to respond right now, not just companies that match filters.
I say "Find hiring signals for this week's Clay export"
Claude Scans Clay and LinkedIn via MCP for roles matching our criteria. Companies with active signals surface to the top. Everyone else drops to Tier 3.
Skill 03
Copy Generation
Outreach sequences adapted to the specific signal and persona. Not templates.
I say "Write the sequence for this hypothesis" (ICP + signal + angle)
Claude Generates a 3-step sequence tied to the actual signal and persona. Not a template. Copy that references what's happening in that company right now.
Skill 04
Campaign Architecture
Set up a full campaign in your sending platform from a single instruction.
I say "Set up the SDR-hiring hypothesis in PlusVibe"
Claude Creates the campaign, names it by hypothesis, uploads the sequence, assigns the contact list and sending schedule. Done.
Skill 05
Reply Analysis
Turn a week of replies into optimization decisions without reading every response manually.
I say "What came in this week?"
Claude Reads every reply in the inbox via MCP. Positive, negative, out of office: all categorized. Returns a summary: what worked, what didn't, one suggested copy change.
