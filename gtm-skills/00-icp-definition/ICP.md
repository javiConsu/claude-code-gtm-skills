## Signal Priority (from highest to lowest)

1. Champion / contact changed jobs → highest intent, warm intro opportunity
2. Company just raised funding → budget available, growth mandate
3. Hiring for roles our product replaces → budget + urgency + pain acknowledged
4. New C-level hired → new leaders buy new tools
5. Competitor mention in community → actively evaluating options
6. Community pain signal (Reddit / forums) → problem acknowledged publicly
